One image for Friday 20 November 2026, laid out as a newspaper front page.

The lead photograph shows Cyclone Akvan coming ashore near Gopalpur
on the Odisha coast, with 300,000 people evacuated as winds reached
110 km/h. Five panels underneath it carry the rest of the day:

- Anthropic's shares close up 17% on their debut, after a record
  $81.3bn listing.
- COP31 closes in Antalya with a $120bn deal on the table.
- A Houthi missile damages a landmark tower in Riyadh.
- Iran steps up tanker attacks in the Strait of Hormuz.
- European allies meet in Berlin and say they are ready for talks
  with Russia.

The black strip at the foot adds five more items: Trump's White House
press conference, a Venezuela–US military deal, a migrant boat sinking
off Senegal with dozens missing, the end of the Gaza ground operation,
and Zverev reaching the ATP Finals semi-finals.

`main.py` is the entrypoint: it copies `entry.png` (JPEG, 768x1024,
about 222 KB) into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`), checks
the copy byte for byte against the source, and refuses anything that
is not a complete JPEG under the 1 MiB cap. Running it twice writes
identical bytes.

To try it locally:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
